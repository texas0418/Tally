APP NAME (30 chars max)
Tally: Bill Splitter & Tip

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 chars max)
Scan the receipt, assign each dish, and split the bill fairly to the cent.

FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 chars max)
-----------------------------------------------------
Split the restaurant bill by what each person actually ordered — not a lazy even split.

Tally scans the receipt with your camera, reads the line items right on your phone, and lets you assign each dish to a person with a tap. Tax and tip divide proportionally, so the friend who ordered the steak and two cocktails pays their share — and the friend who had a side salad pays theirs. Fair to the cent.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Scan the receipt (or add items by hand)
2. Tap to assign each item to a person — shared plates split evenly among whoever shared
3. Set the tip, see everyone's total, done before the check hits the table

WHY TALLY
• Receipt scanning happens entirely on your device — the photo never leaves your phone
• Tax and tip split proportionally by what each person ordered
• Shared appetizers? Assign one item to several people and it divides evenly
• Works offline — basements, patios, airplanes, no signal needed
• No account, no sign-up, no ads
• Clean, fast, and built for the moment the check arrives

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Tally keeps everything on your phone. No cloud, no tracking, no selling your data. The receipt photo is processed on-device and isn't uploaded anywhere.

Perfect for dinners with friends, group trips, work lunches, birthday dinners, and anyone tired of "let's just split it evenly" when they ordered water and an appetizer.

Download Tally and make the end of dinner the easiest part.
